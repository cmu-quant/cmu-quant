Who We Are

The CMU Quant Club focuses on quantitative finance, the study of financial markets through advanced mathematical and computational techniques. We host events that help undergraduates learn about and connect with the industry: mock interviews, resume workshops, lecture series, seminars, and our flagship competitions.
