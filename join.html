Join Us

Quant Club does not host general body meetings or regular workshops. Instead, we focus on high-impact, high-quality events. Our flagship events are in the spring, and throughout the year we run industry treks and other events as opportunities arise. We’d love to have you join us!

Join the mailing list to find out about events as they come up. Events are free to CMU students and announced through the mailing list, where you can apply to attend. For deeper involvement with the club, apply for our Slack community below.
