About

Established at Carnegie Mellon

Quant Finance
for CMU
Students

The CMU Quant Club focuses on the study of quantitative finance, a branch of finance that uses advanced mathematical and computational techniques. We host events that help students learn about and connect with the quantitative finance industry: mock interviews, resume workshops, lecture series, seminars, and our flagship events.

Quant Club does not host general body meetings or regular workshops. Instead, we focus on high-impact, high-quality events. Our flagship events are in the spring, and throughout the year we run industry treks and other events as opportunities arise. We’d love to have you join us!

Competitions

Flagship competitions including the Market Making Game (previously sponsored by Jane Street) and Quantathon (previously sponsored by Goldman Sachs). Over $10K in prizes offered through firm sponsorships.

Network

Direct connections to recruiters and professionals at leading quantitative trading firms through mock interviews, workshops, and firm-hosted sessions.

Industry Insights

Annual treks to New York and Chicago for on-site firm visits and direct conversations with practitioners across the quantitative finance landscape.

Community

A selective Slack for members seeking deeper involvement — technical discussions, shared resources, and connections to the CMU quant network.
